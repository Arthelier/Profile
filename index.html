About Writer

The mind behind the stories

A writer who enjoys writing in the thriller, dark, and psychological thriller genres. Someone who also loves coding and building websites. Only started writing at the end of 2025, so the craft of writing still needs a lot of developing.

Began by writing the character Gathur Delano Kafka with a neo-noir and time-lapse genre (but ended up going around in circles with the politics and thriller), then wrote the character Malvier Hartmann as a way to vent his criminal mind and his black-flag side. Then continued creating several other characters (still not perfect).
